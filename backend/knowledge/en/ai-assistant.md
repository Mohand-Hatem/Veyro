# The AI assistant

## What it does
The assistant answers questions about how Veyro works. Inside a workspace, the quick panel can also draft a task for you. It only talks about Veyro and managing your work; other topics get a short refusal.

## It proposes, you decide
When you ask it to create a task, it drafts a proposal that names the project. Nothing changes until you confirm. Confirming or rejecting a proposal does not use a message.
Only people who can create tasks in that project (project admins, workspace admins and the account owner) get a proposal. Members get answers and explanations.

## Daily messages
Each answer or proposal uses one message from your daily limit. The limit follows the plan of the account you own: Free 3 messages a day, Pro 15, Enterprise 25 and Epic 50. People who only belong to someone else's workspace get 3. The limit renews at 00:00 UTC. If the assistant fails to answer, the message is not counted. Sending the same request twice by mistake is not charged twice.

## What it can see
The assistant answers help questions from the Veyro help documents. It does not yet read your projects, tasks or people, and it says so if you ask for them.
