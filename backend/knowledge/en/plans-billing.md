# Plans and billing

## Plan limits
Limits apply to the whole account, across all its workspaces.
- Free: 1 workspace and 3 projects.
- Pro: 3 workspaces and 7 projects, 9 US dollars a month.
- Enterprise: 10 workspaces and 15 projects, 19 US dollars a month.
- Epic: unlimited workspaces and 25 projects, 29 US dollars a month.
Archived projects count toward the project limit. Deleted projects do not. An empty workspace uses no project slots.

## Who sees billing
Only the account owner sees billing, changes the plan and reads the usage. Workspace admins, project admins and members do not see billing. If the plan blocks something, ask the account owner.

## Paying and changing plan
The owner pays through a secure checkout page and can switch between paid plans from the Billing page. A plan becomes active only after the payment is confirmed by the payment provider, not by returning to the app. Billing is monthly.

## Failed payment and downgrade
If a payment fails, everything keeps working for 7 days of grace. After that the account falls back to Free limits. Nothing is ever deleted because of a downgrade. The account owner must choose which projects stay editable, and until they do, projects stay read-only. After an upgrade the owner chooses which read-only projects to reactivate.

## Cancelling
Cancelling keeps the paid benefits until the end of the period that is already paid.

## Deleting the account
Only the account owner can delete the account. It asks for the password and the typed workspace slug, and it removes all workspaces, projects and tasks.
