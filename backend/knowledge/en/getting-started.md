# Getting started

## From sign-up to the first task
There are four short stops, and you can stop at any point and continue later.
1. Register with your email and a password, or continue with Google or GitHub.
2. Verify your email with the 6-digit code. The code expires after 10 minutes and can be sent again.
3. Choose: create your own workspace, or enter an invitation code to join an existing one.
4. Create the first project and add the first task.

## Who creates what
If you create the workspace yourself you become its first admin. If you arrive by invitation you land in a ready workspace and are already added to your projects.

## Your profile
In your profile you choose your name, language (English or Arabic), theme (light, dark or system) and password.
