# Tasks

## Status path
A task moves through To do, In progress, In review and Done. You can step back one stage. A task cannot jump from To do straight to Done, because it has to be reviewed first.
Cancelled is possible from any open status. Done and Cancelled are final until an admin reopens the task.

## Who can move a task
Project admins, workspace admins and the account owner can move any task, including cancelling it. A member can only move tasks assigned to them and can never cancel.

## Reopen a finished task
To continue a Done or Cancelled task, a project admin or a workspace admin reopens it and must give a reason of at least 5 characters. The reason is added to the task comments and to the audit trail. A task in an archived project cannot be reopened.

## Priority, due date and assignee
A task has a priority (Low, Medium, High or Urgent; Medium by default), an optional due date and at most one assignee. The assignee must be a member of the project. A task past its due date that is not Done or Cancelled shows as overdue.

## Deleting and restoring tasks
Deleted tasks wait 30 days in the trash. An admin can restore them during that time, if the project is active. After 30 days they are removed for good.

## Comments
Every project member can comment on a task. Authors can edit or delete their own comments, and workspace admins can delete any comment.
