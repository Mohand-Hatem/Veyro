# Roles and permissions

## The four roles
- Account Owner: owns the account. Creates workspaces, chooses the plan, sees billing and the account history, and can delete the account.
- Workspace Admin: runs one workspace. Invites, removes and changes the role of members, creates, archives, deletes and restores projects, and manages every task in the workspace. Cannot see billing.
- Project Admin: runs one project. Manages the project members, creates, assigns, edits, reopens and deletes its tasks. Cannot archive or delete the project and cannot invite people to the workspace.
- Member: sees the projects they were added to, moves the tasks assigned to them, and comments.

## What a Member cannot do
A member cannot create, assign or delete tasks. A member cannot cancel a task or move a task that is not assigned to them. A member cannot see projects they were not added to.

## Workspace roles and project roles
Workspace roles (Admin, Member) are separate from project roles (Project Admin, Member). The person who creates a project becomes its Project Admin and is protected: other project admins cannot demote or remove them. Nobody can change their own project role or remove themselves. Workspace admins and the account owner act as project admins on every project.
