# Privacy and the audit trail

## Separate teams
Workspaces are separate and a project you were not added to answers "not found". The assistant only sees what you are allowed to see.

## Audit trail
Important changes are written to an audit trail that nobody can edit or delete. Workspace admins read the workspace trail: who did what, and when. Only the account owner reads the account trail. Project admins and members do not see the audit trail.

## Contact and help
The Contact page works without signing in. Send a message about a question, a problem, billing or feedback and the team reads it.
