# How Veyro is organised

## The four layers
Veyro keeps teams apart and work in order. An account holds workspaces, a workspace holds projects, and a project holds tasks.
- Account: the plan and the billing. One person, the Account Owner, owns it.
- Workspace: a private space for one team. People join by invitation only.
- Project: a private board. Only people added to the project see it. Workspace admins and the account owner see every project.
- Task: one piece of work with an assignee, a due date, a priority and a status.

## Workspaces stay separate
Belonging to the same account does not give anyone access to other workspaces. A project you were not added to simply answers "not found".
